Centripetal Force is the inward force from [[Centripetal Acceleration]]. For example, if we are spinning an object with a rope, the Centripetal Force is the tension of the rope. Another example is gravity for an satellite in orbit.

We can get centripetal force from
$$
\vec{F} = m\left[ \frac{ d^{2}r }{ dt^{2} }  - r\omega^{2} \right]\hat{r} +m \left[ 2\frac{ dr }{ dt } \omega + r\alpha \right]\hat{\theta}
$$
Assuming constant radius and $\omega$, we get
$$
\vec{F} = -mr\omega^{2}\,\hat{r}
$$
If we have Centripetal Force $\vec{F}_{C}$,
$$
\begin{align}
\vec{F}_{C}  = - ma\,\hat{r}  & = -mr\omega^{2} \,\hat{r} \\
a & = r\omega^{2}
\end{align}
$$
We can also rewrite this as
$$
F_{C} = m \frac{v^{2}}{r}
$$
