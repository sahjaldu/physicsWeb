**Newton's Laws:**
1. Every body continues in its state of rest, or of uniform motion in a straight line, unless it is compelled to change that state by forces impressed upon it.
$$
\vec{F}_{\text{net}}=0 \leftrightarrow \frac{d}{dt} \vec{v}=0
$$
2. The change of motion of an object is proportional to the force impressed; and is made in the direction of the straight line in which the force is impressed.
$$
\vec{F}_{\text{net}}=m\vec{a} \leftrightarrow \vec{F}_{\text{net}}=m\frac{d}{dt} \vec{v} \leftrightarrow  \vec{F}_\text{net} = \frac{d}{dt} (ma)
$$
3. To every action, there is always opposed an equal reaction; or, the mutual actions of two bodies upon each other is always equal, and directed to contrary parts.
$$
\vec{F}_{AB}=-\vec{F}_{BA}
$$
